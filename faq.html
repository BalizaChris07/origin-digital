FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often from small business owners.

How do you price?

We build a custom quote based on the scope of your project. A simple site costs less than a complex one. We talk through your needs first, then give you a clear number.

What is the timeline?

Project timelines depend on the size and complexity of the website. We establish the scope and key milestones before development begins so you know what to expect.

Will it be mobile-friendly?

Every site we ship works on phones and tablets. The layout adapts cleanly to any screen. Your customers get a good experience wherever they find you.

Can you update my site?

Yes. We can refresh an existing site or rebuild parts of it when needed. We keep what still works well and improve the parts that are outdated, unclear, or difficult to use.

What do you need?

Your logo, brand colors, content, and project goals are helpful starting points. If some materials aren't ready yet, we can identify what's still needed before the project begins.

Still have questions?

We have answers. Reach out and we will talk through it.
